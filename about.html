About This Site

This website is created for Indian girls and women with PCOS/PMOS who are confused by generic diet and exercise advice. It focuses only on PCOS/PMOS and irregular periods, using Indian foods and home‑friendly workouts.

This website provides general lifestyle information for PCOS/PMOS. It does not replace diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.

  • Always consult a gynecologist or endocrinologist for diagnosis, medications, and personalized advice.
  • If your periods are very irregular, absent for months, or you have severe symptoms, please see a doctor.
