7‑Day Indian PCOS Meal Plan

This plan uses low‑glycemic Indian foods, adequate protein, and high fiber to support insulin sensitivity and hormonal balance. Adjust portions based on your hunger, activity, and doctor’s advice.

Key Principles

  • Prefer low‑GI carbs: bajra, jowar, ragi, oats, brown rice, whole wheat, millets, dals, whole legumes.
  • Include protein at every meal: dals, chana, rajma, paneer, tofu, eggs, chicken, fish, dahi.
  • Load up on fiber: lots of sabzi, salads, whole fruits (not juice).
  • Use healthy fats: nuts, seeds, mustard/olive/groundnut oil in moderation.
  • Keep regular meal times; avoid skipping breakfast.

7‑Day Plan

Day 1

  • Breakfast: Moong dal chilla (2) with mint chutney + 1 katori dahi
  • Lunch: Kachumber salad + rajma (1 katori) + bhindi sabzi + 2 bajra rotis + chaas
  • Snack: Roasted chana (small bowl) + 5 almonds
  • Dinner: Palak paneer (light) + 1 jowar roti

Day 2

  • Breakfast: Vegetable besan dhokla (2–3) with dahi
  • Lunch: Salad + moong dal + lauki sabzi + 1 small katori brown rice + dahi
  • Snack: Buttermilk + small bowl of sprouts
  • Dinner: Grilled fish/tofu/paneer + sautéed vegetables

Day 3

  • Breakfast: 2 boiled eggs or paneer bhurji + 1 multigrain roti
  • Lunch: Salad + chana masala + tinda sabzi + 2 millet rotis + chaas
  • Snack: Fruit (apple/guava) + handful of nuts
  • Dinner: Dal + mixed veg sabzi + 1–2 bajra rotis

Day 4

  • Breakfast: Sprouts chaat with dahi + 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • Lunch: Salad + masoor dal + cabbage‑peas sabzi + 2 bajra rotis + dahi
  • Snack: Green tea + roasted makhana / boiled chana
  • Dinner: Tofu/paneer bhurji + big salad + 1 small jowar roti

Day 5

  • Breakfast: Vegetable omelette (2 eggs) or besan chilla
  • Lunch: Salad + rajma + bhindi sabzi + 1 small katori brown rice + chaas
  • Snack: Dahi + fruit (berries/papaya)
  • Dinner: Chicken curry (less oil) or chole + sabzi + 1 multigrain roti

Day 6

  • Breakfast: Oats upma with lots of veggies + peanuts
  • Lunch: Salad + dal + lauki/tori sabzi + 2 millet rotis + dahi
  • Snack: Buttermilk + handful of nuts
  • Dinner: Paneer/tofu stir‑fry with veggies + 1 small roti or quinoa

Day 7

  • Breakfast: Moong dal cheela or stuffed paratha (multigrain, less oil) + dahi
  • Lunch: Rajma/chole + mixed sabzi + salad + 1–2 bajra rotis
  • Snack: Fruit + seeds/nuts
  • Dinner: Light dal + sabzi + 1 roti or small bowl brown rice

General Tips

  • Pair carbs with protein + fat (e.g., fruit + nuts, roti + dal + sabzi).
  • Avoid sugary drinks; prefer water, chaas, unsweetened tea.
  • Focus on consistency, not perfection.

This plan is general information and not medical advice. Discuss with your doctor or dietitian, especially if you have other conditions (diabetes, thyroid, etc.).
