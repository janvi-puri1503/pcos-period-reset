What is PCOS/PMOS?

  • Common hormonal condition in Indian women affecting periods, skin, hair, and weight.
  • Typical signs: irregular or missing periods, acne, excess hair growth, hair thinning, weight gain.
  • Often linked to insulin resistance and inflammation.

How This Site Helps

  • Indian, low‑GI meal plans (veg & non‑veg) focused on PCOS/PMOS.
  • Home workouts for PCOS – no gym needed, minimal equipment.
  • Free tools: simple period tracker, meal & workout checklists.

This is not a substitute for medical advice. Please consult a gynecologist or endocrinologist for diagnosis and treatment, especially if your periods are very irregular or absent.
