4‑Week PCOS Home Workout Plan

Designed for Indian women with PCOS/PMOS, focusing on insulin sensitivity, strength, and stress management. No gym required; minimal equipment (water bottles or light dumbbells optional).

Weekly Structure

  • Mon: Strength A (Lower body + core) – ~30 min
  • Tue: Zone‑2 walk 30 min + 10 min yoga
  • Wed: Strength B (Upper body + core) – ~30 min
  • Thu: Zone‑2 walk 30 min + breathing (Anulom Vilom)
  • Fri: Strength C (Full body) – ~30 min
  • Sat: Longer walk 45–60 min
  • Sun: Rest + gentle stretching / Yoga Nidra

Zone‑2 walk = brisk but you can still talk in full sentences.

Strength A – Lower Body + Core

Do 2–3 rounds. Rest 60 seconds between exercises.

  • Bodyweight squats – 12–15 reps
  • Glute bridges – 12–15 reps
  • Reverse lunges – 10 reps each leg
  • Calf raises – 15–20 reps
  • Plank – 20–40 seconds
  • Bird‑dog – 10 reps each side

Finish with 5 minutes of stretching.

Strength B – Upper Body + Core

Do 2–3 rounds.

  • Incline push‑ups (wall/bed) – 8–12 reps
  • Dumbbell/bottle rows – 12 reps each arm
  • Shoulder press (water bottles) – 12 reps
  • Triceps dips (chair/bed) – 8–12 reps
  • Dead bug or leg raises – 10–12 reps
  • Side plank – 15–30 seconds each side

Strength C – Full Body (Lighter)

Do 2 rounds.

  • Squats – 12 reps
  • Step‑ups (sturdy chair/step) – 10 reps each leg
  • Push‑ups (knees/wall) – 8–10 reps
  • Hip hinge / good mornings (light weight) – 12 reps
  • Plank – 20–30 seconds
  • Cat–cow + child’s pose – 1–2 minutes

4‑Week Progression

  • Weeks 1–2: 2 rounds per strength session, slow and controlled.
  • Weeks 3–4: 3 rounds; slightly faster or add light weights/bands.

Yoga & Breathing (2–4 times/week)

  • Surya Namaskar (slow) – 3–5 rounds
  • Anulom Vilom – 5–10 minutes
  • Bhramari or simple breathing – 5 minutes

If you feel pain (not just muscle effort), stop and rest. Consult your doctor before starting if you have other health issues.
